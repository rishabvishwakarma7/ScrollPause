How to install

1

Download the APK

Tap the download button above. The file saves to your Downloads folder.

2

Allow unknown sources

When you tap the APK to install, Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser or Files app. This is normal for apps not from the Play Store.

3

Open ScrollPause

Follow the onboarding flow. It explains what the app does and walks you through the two permissions.

4

Enable Accessibility

The onboarding button takes you to Android Settings. Find "ScrollPause Reel Counter" and enable it.

5

Open Instagram Reels

The floating counter appears when you enter the Reels viewer. That's it — ScrollPause is running.

🔒 Privacy

ScrollPause stores only reel counts, session durations, and your preferences — all on your device.

It does not read messages, captions, usernames, or any personal content from Instagram.

It has no internet permission. No data leaves your phone. You can delete everything at any time in Settings.
